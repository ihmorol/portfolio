# Material Symbols Outlined subset

`material-symbols-subset.woff2` is a modified subset of Google's Material
Symbols Outlined variable font. Copyright 2026 Google LLC. All Rights Reserved.

Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
License: Apache License 2.0, reproduced in `LICENSE` alongside this file.

Modifications: removed unused icon ligatures, glyphs, and hinting with fontTools.
The original FILL and wght variable axes and copyright metadata are retained.
The accompanying generator is `scripts/generate_material_symbols_subset.py`.
